About

2 Sides Challenge is a fast-paced, brain-teasing puzzle game where you must connect matching shapes using both hands simultaneously. It sounds simple, but as the clock ticks down, you'll need lightning-fast reflexes and perfect coordination to beat your high score.

Features

  • Dual-Handed Gameplay Swipe and connect shapes on both sides of the screen at the exact same time.
  • Race Against Time Be quick! You only have a few seconds to find and connect the matching pairs.
  • Global Leaderboards Compete with players worldwide via Game Center and prove you have the fastest hands.
  • Endless Fun The game gets progressively harder, challenging your brain and reflexes.
